Sunarc Privacy Policy

Nothing leaves your phone.

This app does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising.

Your location is used only to work out where the sun and the moon are from where you stand. It is never uploaded.

The camera and the motion sensors are used only for the augmented-reality view, on the device. Nothing is recorded.

Place search and map tiles come from Apple Maps. That is the only network traffic the app causes.

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. Saved places stay in the app’s own storage. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.
