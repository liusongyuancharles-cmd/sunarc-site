Common questions

  • How accurate are the times? Sunrise, sunset and twilight are computed on the phone to within a minute of the reference almanac for the chosen place and date.
  • Past-midnight times. In high latitudes an evening event can fall after midnight; it is shown with +1 and still belongs to that day.
  • Reminders. Reminders use the place you have chosen and refresh every day on the phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • The AR view needs the camera and motion sensors. On devices without them the compass and the times still work.
  • Requirements. iOS 26 or later. Shooting plans need Apple Intelligence.
